## How we work

1. **Trial project.** One clear problem, a fixed scope, a working result in weeks.
2. **Retainer.** Ongoing builds, support and improvements on a monthly plan.
3. **You own it.** Builds start in our repos and transfer to your accounts in one handover.

Every week you get a short update: done, next, and anything we need from you.
